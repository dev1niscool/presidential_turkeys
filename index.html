1947

The presentation begins

The modern National Thanksgiving Turkey presentation begins with Harry S. Truman. A presentation, however, was not a pardon.

1963

Kennedy spares his guest

John F. Kennedy sends his turkey back to the farm. An act of mercy, but not yet an annual presidential ritual.

1987

A presidential punch line

Ronald Reagan jokes about pardoning a turkey named Charlie, two years before the formal tradition begins.

1989

The pardon becomes official

George H. W. Bush grants his turkey a presidential pardon. Every president since has carried on the ceremony.

SMALL ARCHIVE. BIG BIRDS.

History, with a side of gratitude.

The Presidential Turkey Atlas is an independent collection of a wonderfully peculiar American tradition. We gather the names, places, photographs, and small stories behind each annual pardon.

Records draw on White House transcripts, presidential libraries, the White House Historical Association, and the birds’ retirement homes. Sources and photo credits are included in each profile. Unknown names and missing photographs are labeled, never filled in with guesses.

Coverage: annual formal pardons, 1989–2026. Historical presentations appear separately above. This is an independent project, not an official government website.
